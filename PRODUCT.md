# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: AI researchers, engineering leaders, technical recruiters, and potential collaborators seeking an AI researcher and systems builder who bridges machine learning, computer vision, cybersecurity, and hardware/IoT.

## Product Purpose

Establish technical authority and showcase Muhammed Arshad's portfolio of work, engineering rigor, and builder philosophy. The goal is to turn technical visitors into collaborators, employers, and co-builders.

## Positioning

Unlike generic corporate developer portfolios with templated cards and gradient buttons, this portfolio presents itself as a living cyber-tactical instrument—featuring real-time canvas ASCII rendering, WebGL particle terrain, live frequency oscillators, and militaristic HUD interaction mechanics.

## Operating Context

Evaluated across high-resolution desktop viewports and mobile web devices. Visitors review project capabilities, interact with technical animations, inspect GitHub source repositories, and reach out via email or socials.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities:
  - Real-time ASCII art rendering engine on canvas with cursor-proximity glitch displacement.
  - Interactive WebGL Three.js terrain particle wave with sonar ripple physics.
  - Split-column vertical HUD project showcase with synchronized telemetry, status indicators, and SVG waveforms.
  - Interactive 3D tilt micro-physics and custom viewport reticle tracking.
  - Zero-dependency framework footprint: pure HTML5, vanilla JS, custom CSS, and GSAP/Lenis.
- Constraints:
  - Strict monochromatic palette discipline (Zinc, Bone White, Tactical Stone Grey).
  - Heavy compositor layers (3D canvas, grid, noise, custom reticle) must gracefully gate on mobile and touch devices.
  - Zero layout jumping or mobile viewport issues (`100dvh` viewport stability).
  - High contrast readability (WCAG AA compliant).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Muhammed Arshad (ARSHAD.)
- Voice: Precise, deliberate, technical, engineering-first. No corporate buzzwords or AI hype copy.
- Palette: High-contrast monochromatic tactical dark theme (#080807 background, #E8E8E3 bone white, #8E8E88 tactical stone grey).
- Typography: Display in Space Grotesk; technical data, telemetries, and metadata in JetBrains Mono.

## Product Principles

1. Instrument Over Template: Every surface should feel like an active, calibrated piece of cybernetic equipment, not a standard web template.
2. Performance Under Pressure: Visual richness must never compromise frame rate, viewport stability, or mobile usability.
3. Radical Focus: Monochromatic palette discipline ensures the user's focus remains on code, projects, and architecture.
4. Tactile Feedback: Every interactive touchpoint provides instantaneous, analog-like feedback through reticles, sound-waveforms, or cryptographic text scrambling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigability with visible focus rings.
- Contrast ratio on secondary and technical text adheres to WCAG AA (≥ 4.5:1).
- Touch devices receive native, fluid touch scrolling with touch-friendly targets, automatically bypassing desktop mouse-tracking overhead.
- Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` for animated canvas, film grain, and kinetic loops.
