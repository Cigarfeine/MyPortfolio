Mnemosyne: academic synthesis & exam intelligence.

An autonomous academic synthesis engine analyzing historical university question repositories to predict question frequencies, isolate syllabus dependencies, and synthesize high-yield revision dossiers.

View GitHub Repository ↗
  • LLM Synthesis
  • Vector Embeddings
  • PDF Document Pipeline
  • Deterministic RAG

The Engine.

University students and researchers face massive repositories of unstructured exam papers, lecture notes, and textbook PDFs. Mnemosyne parses dense technical literature through an automated multi-stage ingestion pipeline.

By extracting semantic embeddings from curriculum syllabi and pairing them with frequency matrices of past examination prompts, Mnemosyne builds an evolving knowledge graph that flags high-probability concepts, identifies prerequisite gaps, and renders structured Markdown revision manuals.

The Bottleneck, The Solution.

Bottleneck

Hallucination in Generic Language Models

Off-the-shelf generative models frequently hallucinate syllabus requirements, invent non-existent course topics, and fail to calculate historical topic distribution weights.

Breakthrough

Grounded Vector Retrieval & Frequency Scoring

Every synthesized output is strictly bounded by deterministic RAG pipelines, linking every summarized concept back to exact textbook page coordinates and past exam session IDs.
