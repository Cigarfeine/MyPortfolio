Hands on the metal, abstracting nothing.

I operate with a systems-first mindset. When implementing agentic AI or computer vision, understanding the hardware limitations, serial latency, and cache hierarchy is what differentiates a fragile demo from a mission-critical tool.

Whether it's writing ESP32 firmware for ultrasonic transducers, deploying vLLM inference microservices on Linux GPU clusters, or profiling WebGL fragment shaders, I prefer direct ownership of the full technical stack.

How I engineer.

Every engagement follows a rigorous cycle: reverse-engineering existing barriers, isolating the mathematical or structural bottlenecks, prototyping minimal deterministic proof-of-concepts, and hardening for production deployment.

No unnecessary dependencies. No bloated libraries. Strictly performant, maintainable, and verified code.
